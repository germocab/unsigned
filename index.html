Information version 1.3

What is this project?

Unsigned is a social experiment: a shared universe of anonymous bubbles. Anyone with the link can write a short message and publish it. Everyone sees the same bubbles, and nobody knows who wrote which one.

What is the goal?

To see what people say when there's no name, no profile and no fear of being recognized. The aim is to read honest, unfiltered thoughts from a group of people, and to let everyone speak freely and safely.

How it works

  • You have one chance to publish, so choose your words wisely. Messages can be up to 1000 characters.
  • Every bubble shows its number, in the order it was published. When you publish, you earn a colorful character as a reward.
  • Only you can tell which bubble is yours: it gets a white ring on your device, and you can see your character anytime with the "My character" button.
  • The limit is per browser. There are no accounts, so it can't be enforced perfectly. Clearing your browser data or using another device would allow another post, so please keep it fair.

Privacy

This website doesn't ask for or store your name, email, phone number or any account. It has no login. Only the text of each bubble and the time it was published are saved online. Your own browser keeps a small note on your device saying you've posted, plus a copy of your bubble, so the site can remember you and show your character. Like any website, the hosting and database services may keep basic technical logs that this project doesn't control.

Please read before writing

  • Don't share private information: no phone numbers, email addresses, usernames, social media accounts, links, or real names of people.
  • Bubbles can't be edited or deleted once published, so think before you post.
  • Messages that contain private information are blocked automatically, but the filter isn't perfect. You are responsible for what you write.
  • Be kind. Don't use this space to harass or threaten anyone.
  • Everything here is written by anonymous people. The project doesn't verify or endorse any bubble.
